## 🎬 EDITING QUICK-START

### IN CAPCIT - 5 MIN FORMULA

1. **Import video** (trim to 20-30s)
2. **Add audio** (trending sound of the week)
3. **Add text** (4x overlays, timed to music beats)
4. **Add transition** (1x zoom or fade between shots)
5. **Color grade** (sat +10%, brightness +2%)
6. **Export** (1080x1920)

### CAPCIT KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
- Space bar = Play/Pause
- R = Rotate
- V = Volume
- T = Text
- C = Color/Effects
- Export = Quality 1080x1920
