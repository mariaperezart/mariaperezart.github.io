## 📱 PLATFORM-SPECIFIC OPTIMIZATION

### FOR INSTAGRAM REELS
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds (sweet spot)
- **Audio:** Trending sounds (Instagram Sounds library)
- **Text:** 3-4 overlays max (not cluttered)
- **Hook:** Visual first 0.5s (no text initially)
- **CTA:** "Swipe up for more" / "Follow for next"
- **Colors:** Slightly boost saturation (+5-10%)

### FOR TIKTOK
- **Duration:** 15-25 seconds (algorithm favors shorter)
- **Audio:** TRENDING sound (this week, not last month)
- **Text:** Bold, large, timed to music beats
- **Hook:** Text + audio hook together (0-1s)
- **CTA:** "Like if you'd try", "Tag someone", "Comment 👇"
- **Colors:** High contrast, punchy saturation (+15-20%)
- **Speed:** Mix normal (1x) with 0.75x/1.25x for drama

### FOR YOUTUBE SHORTS
- **Duration:** 30-45 seconds (can be longer)
- **Audio:** Original music (not required trending)
- **Text:** Professional, clean typography
- **Hook:** "Wait for it...", "This will shock you"
- **CTA:** "Subscribe for daily content"
- **Style:** Cinematic over viral

## 🎨 EDITING PRINCIPLES FOR VIRALITY

### TRANSITIONS
- **Fade:** Use for emotional moments
- **Zoom:** Use for impact/reveal
- **Cut:** Use for beat-matching (TikTok)
- **Spin:** Avoid (too cheesy)
- **Whip:** Use sparingly (can backfire)

**RULE:** Max 1 transition every 3-4 seconds. More = distraction.

### TEXT OVERLAY
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif (Arial, Montserrat, Bebas)
- **Size:** Large enough to read on phone
- **Color:** White with black outline (readable on any background)
- **Position:** Follow action (text moves with subject)
- **Duration:** 2-3 seconds per text

### COLOR GRADING
For "Aerial Beauty" Content:
```
Saturation: +5 to +15% (drone already saturated)
Brightness: +2% (don't blow out highlights)
Contrast: +8% (make details pop)
Temperature: +2% warm (golden hour feeling)
Shadows: Lift +3% (keep details visible)
```

### AUDIO STRATEGY
- **TikTok:** ALWAYS use trending sound (2+ million uses)
- **Reels:** Trending or original (Instagram favors variety)
- **YouTube Shorts:** Original music preferred
- **Volume:** Music 100%, voiceover (if any) 70%
- **Timing:** Music peaks at video climax
